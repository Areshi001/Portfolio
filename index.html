About

Engineering AI you can interrogate

I'm a final-year CSE student working mostly in machine learning, ensemble methods, and explainable AI. Most of my projects are pipelines that take messy, multi-modal data and turn it into models someone other than me can read. I've started applying the same approach to LLM and RAG systems.

On GitHub I build at the intersection of AI and design: RAG chatbots, a natural-language-to-SQL tool, and a UI component library. I like the interface side almost as much as the model side.

Most of my work sits in healthcare, environmental, and biology problem sets, and I'm trying to get better at building things that could actually run outside a notebook.

Methodology

Cleaning and structuring data before training even starts, since that's usually where a model's real problems come from.

Architecture

Wiring ML and neural network models into actual software instead of leaving them in a notebook.

Analysis

Reducing high-dimensional data down to something readable enough to actually report or question.
